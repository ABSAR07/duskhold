## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged as in Phases 2 to 4): "Day decisions gain real depth. The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. All bonuses stack in one tested order."

<objective>
Build the one stat-modifier stack every bonus in the game will go through (META-08, D-15), document its order, prove it with single-modifier and combined-modifier tests, and route every modifiable stat the simulation reads through it with no modifier present, so nothing moves. Task 1 is the tracer: the stack, its registry and modifier resource, the document, the tower's damage and range read through it, and the Stats section in the debug overlay, end to end with the goldens unchanged. Task 2 routes every other consumer through it, adds the health-refresh rule and the combined tests.

Purpose: the castle abilities (plan 05-02) and the Shrine buff (plan 05-09) are the first sources; perks, mutators and research (Phases 10 and 12) reuse the same stack, so it is built first and alone (CONTEXT suggested shape).

Output: StatStack, StatModifierDef, StatIds, docs/STAT_MODIFIERS.md, StatOverlaySections, consumer wiring, five test scripts.
</objective>
