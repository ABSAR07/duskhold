## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Day decisions gain real depth. The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. All bonuses stack in one tested order."

<objective>
Build the one requirement mechanism D-03 and D-10 share: a tier that needs the castle at tier N, a plot that needs another spot at tier N, both refused at the build gate with a readable label and the denied bump, validated in data, and safe for the bots. Task 1 is the tracer: on a copy of the prototype map with Tower II gated, the real scene shows "Needs Castle II", refuses the hold for free, and opens the tier once castle II stands. Task 2 adds the validate() checks and the bots' defer-on-locked rule.

Purpose: BLDG-09 (the castle tier gates higher building tiers) and the Field lock of plan 05-07 ride the same check. The shipped top tiers of House, Tower, Barracks, Archery Range and Wall are gated in plan 05-11, in the same commit as the castle-aware bot orders and the balance re-pin, because test_balance_acceptance plays the bots on the shipped map and a gate without the bots' castle purchase would move its pins mid-phase (a dependency, not a difficulty).

Output: requirement fields, BuildRequirements, two rejection reasons, locked label lines, BuildRequirementValidation, the bot defer rule and their tests.
</objective>
