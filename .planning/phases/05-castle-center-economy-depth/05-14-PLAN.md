## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Day decisions gain real depth. The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. All bonuses stack in one tested order."

This plan finishes UAT gap G-05-2 (test 2, BLDG-08) on top of plan 05-13's carry: the three cards the carry cannot make viable are recut, a guard keeps every card landing at every shipped base, and the carry is written into the stacking contract and pinned.

<objective>
Make every card at a tier a viable pick and guard against the defect coming back, per the owner's UAT decision of 2026-10-24 ("all alternatives at an upgrade level are mostly viable options, stronger in particular cases but not overly weak even in a case where its not an ideal option"; Prosperity is too weak).

The resolution choice is plan 05-13's (a per-attacker carry in StatStack; the owner's x100 rescale and the data-only recut of the dead percent cards were rejected there with their reasons). This plan keeps the data-only recut's viability half for the three cards a carry cannot fix: Prosperity and Golden Age's income half are flat dawn gold, and Headsman's Edge's interval cost outweighs its damage against the shipped one-hit kills, so none of them is helped by better rounding.

Task 1 is the tracer: Prosperity's +2 goes from the card file through its data pin and the castle label to the first cases of the recurrence guard, which builds every shipped base from the shipped data. Task 2 recuts Golden Age and Headsman's Edge and completes the guard with the per-hit cards, Golden Age on top of the Shrine synergy and the negative control. Task 3 writes plan 05-13's carry into the contract document and pins it, with every hit site, in test_stat_contract (moved here from plan 05-13 at the second plan check, so each plan stays at or under 10 files; it touches no simulation or data file and can run alone in a fresh executor if this plan's context runs short after Task 2).

Output: three recut cards with their pins and reasons, the recurrence guard, the carry in docs/STAT_MODIFIERS.md with its contract pins, and the per-card before and after table in the SUMMARY. The bar re-measure and the report are plan 05-16's, the full suite and the owner's decisions on the cards the bots cannot reach plan 05-17's.
</objective>
