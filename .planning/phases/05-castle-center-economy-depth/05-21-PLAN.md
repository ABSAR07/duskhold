## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Day decisions gain real depth. The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. All bonuses stack in one tested order."

This plan finishes UAT gap G-05-2 (test 2, BLDG-08) on the rescaled data of plans 05-13 to 05-20: the three cards a resolution fix cannot make viable are recut, and a guard keeps every card landing at every shipped base.

<objective>
Make every card at a tier a viable pick and guard against the defect coming back, per the owner's UAT decision of 2026-10-24 ("all alternatives at an upgrade level are mostly viable options, stronger in particular cases but not overly weak even in a case where its not an ideal option"; Prosperity is too weak).

The resolution half of G-05-2 is the owner's x100 rescale (hard requirement, 2026-10-25), done in plans 05-13 to 05-20; the per-attacker rounding carry first planned is dropped and StatStack keeps its one half-up rounding (D-15). The rescale makes every percent land as written, but it cannot help three cards: Prosperity and Golden Age's income half are flat gold (not rescaled) worth the value times the paying dawns left, and Headsman's Edge's slower blow outweighs its doubled damage against one-hit kills. Those three are recut here in data.

**The hits-to-kill point (for the owner's re-judge, plan 05-24).** A percent of damage changes a kill only when it crosses a whole enemy: Commander's 125 kills a 600 grunt in 5 blows instead of 6 but a 400 skirmisher still in 4; Engineers' honest +25% (375) changes no Tower I kill at all (the old rounding made 3 into 4, a third more, which one-shot 4-health skirmishers); the Shrine synergy alone (360) no longer one-shots a skirmisher, synergy plus Golden Age (435) does. The owner may later raise enemy health relative to damage to make the steps finer.

Task 1 is the tracer: Prosperity's +2 from the card file through its pin and the castle label to the guard's first cases. Task 2 recuts Golden Age and Headsman's Edge, completes the guard and writes the per-card before and after table.

Output: three recut cards with their pins and reasons, the recurrence guard, and the before and after table in the SUMMARY for plan 05-23's report.
</objective>
