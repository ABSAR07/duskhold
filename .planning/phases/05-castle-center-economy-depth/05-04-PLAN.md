The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. Output: mouse picking in CardPicker, CardRowModel.hit_test and the numbers line, twelve first-party SVG icons, three keep scenes, the castle view swap, ShotEconomyScenarios with two shots, and their tests.
</objective>
