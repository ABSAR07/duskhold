## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Day decisions gain real depth. The castle center grows through tiers with run-defining ability picks that gate higher building tiers, and four new economic buildings each pay out on a different income curve. All bonuses stack in one tested order."

This plan and plan 05-14 close UAT gap G-05-2 (test 2, BLDG-08): the twelve cards are distinct and each lands in the stack, but at the shipped numbers some percents round away or overshoot, and the income cards are weak by construction. This plan fixes the rounding; plan 05-14 recuts the weak cards and adds the recurrence guard.

<objective>
Make every percent card land as written at the shipped numbers, per the owner's UAT decision of 2026-10-24 ("percentages and rounding to nearest unit will work more as intended"; "all alternatives at an upgrade level are mostly viable options, stronger in particular cases but not overly weak even in a case where its not an ideal option"; Prosperity is too weak).

**Choice (the owner wrote "perhaps", so the planner chose the alternative that delivers the intent with the smallest blast radius): a per-attacker fractional carry inside StatStack (debug alternative iii), plus a data recut of the three cards a carry cannot make viable (Prosperity, Golden Age's income half, Headsman's Edge) in plan 05-14.** Each attacker (a unit, a tower or Shrine plot, the king, the castle) carries the remainder of its last hit's rounding into its next hit, so +25% on a 1-damage soldier deals 1, 2, 1, 1 and averages exactly 1.25. Data, displays, fixtures and goldens stay as they are; an empty stack and every stat without a modifier read exactly as today.

Rejected alternatives, with reasons:
- **The owner's x100 (or x10) rescale of every health and damage number.** It fixes the rounding of a percent but not the hits-to-kill steps (enemies die in 1 to 6 hits, and a uniform rescale keeps every one of those thresholds, so Engineers' damage half would still change no Tower I kill), and it touches about 20 data files, 5 stress files, 7 fixtures, 10 display sites (50 / 50 would read 5000 / 5000 unless every display divides), about 15 contract tests and every balance pin, and moves full_idle. The carry gives exact averages at the shipped scale with none of that.
- **A data-only recut of the dead cards (debug alternative ii: Commander +1 flat damage, Warded Mail -1 damage taken, Golden Age +1 shrine damage).** Smallest blast, but it removes the percents instead of making them work: Commander would double unit damage instead of adding a quarter, and every later percent on a small base (Phase 6 specialisations, Phase 10 perks, Phase 12 research all go through this stack, D-15) would hit the same trap. Its viability recut is kept, in plan 05-14, for the three cards the carry cannot fix.

Task 1 is the tracer: the carry in StatStack, the per-hit registry, the carry line in the stack's header and Commander's shipped card landing on the shipped footman through the real squad hit path. Task 2 routes the other six per-hit stats through the carry and pins each one through its real hit path.

Output: StatStack.hit_value and StatIds.PER_HIT_STATS, seven hit sites on the carry, the carry line in the head of stat_stack.gd, the carry tests and the card-hit integration test. The carry's contract document and pins (docs/STAT_MODIFIERS.md and test_stat_contract, moved at the second plan check to keep this plan at or under 10 files), the card recut and the recurrence guard are plan 05-14's, the bar re-measure and the report plan 05-16's, the full suite plan 05-17's.
</objective>
