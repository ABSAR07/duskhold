## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "A Godot 4.7.2 project where, on the prototype map, the player rides the king by day and spends scarce gold on Houses and a basic tower, earning income from one day to the next. From the first commit, the agent can lint, test, screenshot, and export the game headlessly on every push."

<objective>
Make the hold-to-build interaction readable and trustworthy (BLDG-02, BLDG-03). The locked decisions implemented here:
- D-07: a world-space spot label above the nearest in-range spot
- D-05: coins that visibly drip from the king into the spot
- D-06: an all-or-nothing hold with a full refund on early release or leaving range
- D-08: a red cost and a denied shake at unaffordable spots, with `hold_denied` as the silent SFX hook until Phase 8

Purpose: the player must always see what holding will do, what it costs, and why nothing happened ("never fail silently", PITFALLS UX table).

Output: the hardened hold controller, the spot label (pure model + scene), the coin-drip VFX, the HUD pending display, and five test files.
</objective>
