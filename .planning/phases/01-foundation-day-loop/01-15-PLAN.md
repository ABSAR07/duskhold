## Design Defaults Chosen

| Choice | Default | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Flight per coin | `clampf(0.9 x gap to the next coin, 0.12, ceiling)` | Coins of every shipped build (costs up to 6) land before the next one leaves. From about the 8th coin of a long hold, the 0.12 s floor makes the stream overlap slightly (at most two drip coins in the air at the 0.08 s interval floor), which reads as speeding up |
| Flight ceiling | 0.9 x `LoopTuning.COIN_DRIP_INTERVAL_MAX_S` (0.27 s) | Derived from D-05's bound, so no second number needs hand-editing (review IN-02). It only binds for the tests' deliberately slow drips |
| Same-frame stagger | `launch_stagger(n) = clampf(0.3 / (n - 1), 0, 0.04)` | The `DawnPayoutVfx.launch_stagger` pattern: a 6-coin cap rush spreads over 0.2 s, and any rush fits in 0.3 s |
| Visual cap per group | 12 coins | Like `DawnPayoutVfx.MAX_COINS`. A 50-coin tier's 26-coin rush draws 12 coins; the HUD and the label still count every coin |
| Sandbox House costs | 15 / 30 / 50 | 15 coins about 2.2 s; 30 coins hit the cap with 6 rushed; 50 coins hit it with 26 rushed (about half, the owner's example) |
