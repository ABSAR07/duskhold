## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "A Godot 4.7.2 project where, on the prototype map, the player rides the king by day and spends scarce gold on Houses and a basic tower, earning income from one day to the next. From the first commit, the agent can lint, test, screenshot, and export the game headlessly on every push."

<objective>
Close the loop. The day ends only through the real hold-to-confirm start-night input (D-11). An enemy-free placeholder night with a night lighting mood and banner (D-12) leads to dawn, where each House pays its tier income (ECON-02) and unspent gold carries over (ECON-07). The next day then begins. Build and upgrade are locked outside the day (BLDG-06).

Purpose: this establishes the exact state machine and lighting states that Phase 2 fills with real nights, rather than replacing them (ROADMAP sequencing rule). Every transition is proven headlessly.

Output: the RunManager loop, StartNightIntent, the start-night hold input, the HUD prompt and banner, lighting moods, overlay loop rows, and five test files.
</objective>
