## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "A Godot 4.7.2 project where, on the prototype map, the player rides the king by day and spends scarce gold on Houses and a basic tower, earning income from one day to the next. From the first commit, the agent can lint, test, screenshot, and export the game headlessly on every push."

<objective>
Add the DEV-03 debug overlay: a keyboard/gamepad toggle and a read-only view of FPS, unit/enemy counts, loop phase, gold and building count. It is built as registered sections so that Phase 2 can add wave state and enemy paths.

Purpose: this gives both the owner's playtests and the automated screenshots (plan 01-10) a view of simulation state (PITFALLS Pitfall 8). Keeping it read-only by construction (RESEARCH Pitfall 5) means it can never become a second source of truth.

Output: the overlay model, scene and script; its instance inside `hud.tscn`; `RunContext` unit/enemy count getters; and two tests.
</objective>
