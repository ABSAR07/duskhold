## Design Defaults Chosen (owner asked for sensible defaults, reported by the orchestrator)

| Choice | Default | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Default camera distance | `offset = Vector3(0, 20.8, 14.3)`: 1.3x the UAT framing (0, 16, 11), same angle (pitch about 55.5 deg down, yaw 0), about 25.2 m from the king instead of 19.4 m | "A bit further away"; debugger range 1.25-1.5x; frames the castle plus the first House ring at spawn |
| Zoom range | `zoom_min = 0.7` (about 17.7 m) to `zoom_max = 1.5` (about 37.9 m, close to 2x the UAT distance), multipliers of `offset` | Zoom in slightly closer than UAT; zoom out far enough to see the whole base core (about 39 x 36 m) |
| Zoom step | Continuous while held: `zoom_speed = 0.6` zoom units per second at full input (default to max in about 0.8 s, min to max in about 1.3 s); the right stick is analog, so a partial push zooms slower | One model for keys and stick; a tap nudges, a hold sweeps |
| Zoom smoothing | No second smoothing stage: the existing follow smoothing (`follow_sharpness = 6.0`, time constant about 0.17 s) eases every zoom change | The rig already lerps toward its target point; zoom only moves that target along the fixed ray |
| Keyboard bindings | `zoom_in`: `=` (`KEY_EQUAL`, 61) and keypad `+` (`KEY_KP_ADD`, 4194437); `zoom_out`: `-` (`KEY_MINUS`, 45) and keypad `-` (`KEY_KP_SUBTRACT`, 4194435). Physical keycodes, values confirmed on the pinned 4.7.2 binary at planning time | No mouse (contract); clear of every existing binding and of Q/R/F/1-4 |
| Gamepad bindings | Right stick Y (`JOY_AXIS_RIGHT_Y`, axis 3): up (-1.0) = `zoom_in`, down (+1.0) = `zoom_out`; deadzone 0.3 | The right stick has no other use under a fixed camera; face buttons, bumpers and D-pad stay free for Phase 3 |
| Label legibility | `SpotLabel` scales itself by camera distance / 16.9 m, never below 1.0 | Keeps the UAT-6-approved on-screen size at every zoom-out level; 16.9 m is the camera-to-label distance under the approved framing |
| Not done | Building collision (debugger "optional") and the minimap | Collision belongs with Phase 4 obstacles (01-04 flagged that the king passes through building views); the minimap is backlog 999.1 |
