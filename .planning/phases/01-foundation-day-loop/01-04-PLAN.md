## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "A Godot 4.7.2 project where, on the prototype map, the player rides the king by day and spends scarce gold on Houses and a basic tower, earning income from one day to the next. From the first commit, the agent can lint, test, screenshot, and export the game headlessly on every push."

<objective>
Complete KING-01 and KING-02 on top of the skeleton's walk/sprint path. This plan adds acceleration and facing, and replaces the king-parented camera with a detached, smoothed, fixed-angle follow rig. It also locks the Input Map contract with a test. That test backs the no-mouse-gameplay rule and D-11's no-conflict rule.

Purpose: riding is the player's whole day-phase interface, and travel time is the cost of every build decision. It must feel right and stay correct as later plans add content.

Output: `camera_rig.gd`, king feel updates, `KingDef` fields, and three test files.
</objective>

  - The king has no stamina.
  - Diagonal speed equals cardinal speed, because `Input.get_vector` normalizes.
  - Stick input below the 0.2 deadzone is ignored.

  - The camera is perspective (FOV 40), yaw 0 with north up, pitched about -55° at an offset of (0, 16, 11).
