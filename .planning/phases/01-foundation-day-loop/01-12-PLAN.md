## Design Defaults Chosen (owner asked for sensible defaults, reported by the orchestrator)

| Choice | Default | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Occlusion technique | Godot's built-in stencil X-Ray preset (`stencil_mode = STENCIL_MODE_XRAY`) on duplicated copies of the king's surface materials | Debugger-verified in Forward+ and Compatibility with no false tint in the open; instance transparency fails in Compatibility; an inverted-depth overlay tints the king's own overlapping parts |
| `stencil_color` | `Color(0.4, 0.9, 1.0, 1.0)`: light cyan, opaque | Probed on the pinned 4.7.2 binary: the generated X-Ray pass is unshaded, alpha-blended and ignores depth, so night lighting (sun energy 0.25, ambient 0.3) cannot dim it. Light cyan contrasts with the grey keep, the House and tower models and the dark-blue night. No part of the visible king uses it (the crown is gold and the collar is a darker blue), so a hidden king reads differently from a visible one |
| Which models | The king only | Buildings must not show through each other; units and enemies can reuse `XRaySilhouette` in later phases |
| Screenshot | New `king_behind_keep` shot (seven shots in total) | A permanent visual record of the fix, locally and as a CI artifact |
