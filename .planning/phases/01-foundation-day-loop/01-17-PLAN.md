## Owner Decision, Cap-Field Fate, and Design Defaults

Binding (owner, UAT G-01-59, amends D-05 again): no hard time limit on a hold; after the acceleration a coin takes at least 0.05 s. D-06 (all-or-nothing, full cost to start, refund on release) and D-09 (numbers in data) are unchanged.

**Cap-field fate: keep the field as a dormant switch, shipped off (Option A from the diagnosis).** `max_build_hold_seconds` stays on `LoopTuning`, and its existing "0 or less means no cap" branch stays in `coin_due_seconds`. The shipped value and the script default become 0.0. Why this and not deleting the field (Option B):
- It is the smaller change. The owner's request becomes two data values and no runtime logic change. The controller and the coin VFX never read the cap, and the no-cap branch is already exercised by `E2eSupport.flat_drip_tuning` and the curve tests.
- No locked decision argues for deletion. D-09 keeps tuning numbers in data, so a cap stays a data-only knob the Phase 2 playtest could turn back on without code.
- Review WR-02 worried that a 0 silently removes the cap. The owner has now made "no cap" the intended state, which reverses that premise. The contract test pins no cap on purpose, so a positive value creeping back in fails a test.
- Deleting would also remove the cap math tests and T-01-24's subject for no behavioural gain. T-01-24 is instead rescoped to "a frame that pays several coins" (a long frame, or the cap if the data ever sets one). Plan 01-18 records that in `01-SECURITY.md`.

Kept defaults and why:

| Field / constant | Value | Why |
|------------------|-------|-----|
| `coin_drip_interval` (first and steady interval) | 0.25 s (unchanged) | Owner value from G-01-58; inside D-05's 0.15-0.3 s range |
| `coin_drip_steady_coins` | 2 (unchanged) | Keeps House I at exactly 0.5 s (UAT G-01-4 minimum) |
| `coin_drip_decay` | 0.9 (unchanged) | Acceleration the owner approved |
| `coin_drip_min_interval` | **0.05 s** (was 0.08) | Owner value, UAT G-01-59; reached at coin 18 |
| `max_build_hold_seconds` | **0.0 = no cap** (was 3.0) | Owner, UAT G-01-59; field kept as a dormant switch |
| `CoinDripVfx.MIN_FLIGHT_SECONDS` | 0.12 s (unchanged, deliberately) | 0.9 x a 0.05 s gap would be a 0.045 s flight, under 3 frames at 60 Hz, which reads as a flicker, not a coin. 0.12 s keeps each coin visible. The cost is up to 3 coins in the air at the floor (2 before); the owner's re-check judges it. Nothing requires changing it, so it stays |
| `CoinDripVfx.MAX_BURST_COINS`, `BURST_WINDOW_SECONDS`, `launch_stagger` | unchanged | Still needed: refunds fly up to the whole paid count back, and any frame longer than 0.05 s (a hitch, or under 20 fps) pays 2+ coins in one frame |
