## Design Defaults Chosen (owner asked for sensible defaults, reported by the orchestrator)

| Choice | Default | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| `coin_drip_interval` | 0.3 s per coin (was 0.2) | Top of D-05's documented 0.15-0.3 s starting range; 1.5x longer, "a bit" longer as asked. Durations: House I 0.6 s, House II 0.9 s, House III 1.5 s, Tower I 1.2 s, Tower II 1.8 s |
| Coin flight cap | `CoinDripVfx.MAX_FLIGHT_SECONDS` 0.27 s (was 0.18) | Keeps each coin in flight for 90% of the interval, the stream feel that passed UAT test 7; at 0.18 a 0.3 s interval would show a 0.12 s gap between coins |
| Minimum-hold floor in code | Not added | It would make House I, House II and Tower I take the same time, breaking D-05's "pricier builds take a little longer" |

<objective>
Close G-01-4: make every build and upgrade hold a little longer by raising the per-coin drip interval from 0.2 s to 0.3 s, and keep the coin stream continuous by raising the coin flight cap to match.

Purpose: the root cause is tuning, not a code defect. Hold time is exactly cost x coin_drip_interval with no floor (`input/build_hold_controller.gd` lines 91-102), and 0.2 s is the fast end of D-05's placeholder range, so the most common builds finished in 0.4 s (House I) and 0.6 s (House II), well under the game's 1.5 s "deliberate" start-night hold. D-09 puts all such numbers in `.tres` data, so the fix is a data change plus the matching visual cap.

Output: updated tuning data and script default, the raised flight cap, a data-contract unit test, and a real-scene timing test.
</objective>
