## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged as in plans 03-01 to 03-12): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

Gap: G-03-8 (03-UAT.md test 8, severity major). With three moves queued, Shift+H cleared the queue and held the squad at once at its centroid, so the D-09 gesture "move here, then hold" cannot be given from the keyboard. Root cause (from the probe and the code): `CommandKeyController._give(verb)` submits `SquadOrderIntent.new(ids, verb)` with the queued flag left false and never reads `command_queue`, while `SelectionController` (right-click move, patrol commit) and `PadCursorController` pass `Input.is_action_pressed(&"command_queue")`. `SquadSystem.apply_order` already accepts a queued hold: the probe's queued hold intent after a move returned OK and the queue read [move, hold]. Only the key path is missing.

<objective>
Make F, H and R (and their D-pad twins) honour the queue key, so Shift+H after a string of Shift right-click moves queues the hold behind the walks and the squad holds at the last point, exactly as D-09 promises. Pin it with real-scene tests on the keyboard and the pad, and confirm it in a real window.

Purpose: closes G-03-8 (UNIT-10's Shift queue, UNIT-05's hold, INPT-01 and INPT-02 parity) with one line of behaviour in the input layer.

Output: the queue-aware `_give` in `input/command_key_controller.gd`, three keyboard cases in `tests/e2e/test_command_keys.gd`, one pad case in `tests/e2e/test_pad_commands.gd`, and real-window evidence in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
