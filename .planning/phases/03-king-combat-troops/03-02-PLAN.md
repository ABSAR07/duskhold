## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
Give squads their life cycle across the loop: losses trickle back during the night at the tier's pace, every squad is whole again at dawn, new seats fill at once by day, and bad unit or squad data is reported instead of armed. The military plots get their pinned numbers, their "Squad of N" label and a model per tier.

Purpose: UNIT-07 and the remaining squad decisions (D-01, D-02, D-03 pins, D-04 by day, D-05, D-14 for dawn) on top of the 03-01 slice, so the HUD and command plans read a squad whose numbers already behave.

Output: trickle and restore in SquadSystem, the squad_restored signal, RunManager's dawn call, MapConfig squad validation, the military data contract, the effect line, six wrapper scenes and catalog entries, and their tests.
</objective>
