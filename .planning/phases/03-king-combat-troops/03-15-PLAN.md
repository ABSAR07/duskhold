## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged as in plans 03-01 to 03-12): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

Gaps (03-UAT.md, all cosmetic, all UI-01):
- G-03-14b (test 14, owner decision 2026-10-13 to fix it now): in hud_day_squads the selected slot's 4 px #8CEBFF border and tinted plate (y 544..614) outrank the unplated start-night prompt (y 624..650), the contract's day focal point. Root cause: StartNightBox draws outlined text straight on the 3D scene while the selected SquadSlot draws an accent border on a plate.
- G-03-13 (test 13): the knocked-out '0 / 50' measures 1.09:1 in a real-window capture. Root cause: `KingWidget._apply_knocked_out` sets `modulate.a = 0.4` on the whole widget, so the #FF4040 HealthLabel dims with the bar and the icon.
- G-03-14 (test 14): 'Night 1 of 8 — 1 enemies left' and 'Night 1: 1 enemies from 1 direction'. Root cause: NIGHT_BANNER, NIGHT_PREVIEW and NIGHT_PREVIEW_ONE in `ui/hud/hud.gd` carry a fixed 'enemies' word. The payout line measured fine in real time (a fast-forward artefact in the 03-12 shots), so it needs no change.

Purpose: closes G-03-14b, G-03-13 and G-03-14 (UI-01: the HUD reads at night, the centre stays clear, the day focal point leads).
