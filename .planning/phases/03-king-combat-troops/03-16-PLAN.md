## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged as in plans 03-01 to 03-12): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

Gap: G-03-11 (03-UAT.md test 11, severity major, owner decision 2026-10-13: retune further now toward all four goals at once). This plan is the first of two: it gives the bots a way to measure the fourth goal ("squads should be worth their gold against towers"), which no report column measures today, and records the baseline. Plan 03-17 then retunes the data against all four targets. G-03-11 is closed only by 03-17.

Root cause recorded in the UAT: plan 03-11 could move only night counts; with costs, squads and towers fixed, raising late nights hurts the army route before the tower route, so the four goals need squad or tower values to move with nights 5 to 8. To steer that search the report needs kills per gold by role.

<objective>
Teach the balance report what each building role bought: the gold spent on military, towers and economy per run, the kills squads and towers made, kills per gold per role and the ratio between them. Then measure today's data with it and open the Round 2 section of the balance report with the owner's four goals as measurable targets.

Purpose: makes "squads are worth their gold against towers" a number the retune (03-17) can hit and the owner can read at the gate (D-08: the bots measure, the owner judges feel).

Output: Tally role spend and run totals in `tools/replay/replay_driver.gd`, summary keys and the squad value table in `tools/replay/balance_report.gd`, `tests/integration/test_balance_value_metric.gd`, the Round 2 targets and baseline in `03-BALANCE-REPORT.md`.
</objective>
