## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
The player reads every squad's state at a glance from a bottom-centre strip: what it is, how many are alive of how many, what it is doing, and when the next replacement arrives. The developer reads the same in the debug overlay.

Purpose: UI-01's "unit group status" and D-23, ahead of the command plans that add selection highlight (03-06) and control-group badges (03-08) to these slots.

Output: SquadOrder constants and the two stance getters, BowIcon, StanceGlyph, SquadSlot (scene and script), SquadStrip, the Squads overlay section, and their tests.
</objective>
