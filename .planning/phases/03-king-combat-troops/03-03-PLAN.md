## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
Lay the HUD's ground rules while the player gains one visible thing: the top-centre "Night N of T" counter. First the stretch setting the UI-SPEC requires lands on its own, with the full suite run before anything builds on it (Pitfall 7). Then one themed HudRoot, the four-size font scale, the outline rule and the protected-centre contract test that every later HUD plan extends.

Purpose: UI-01 needs gold, night out of total, king health, ability cooldown and squad status without clutter (D-21). Gold exists; this plan adds the night counter and the container, theme and contract the king widget (03-04), squad strip (03-05), toast (03-06) and prompts (03-10) build on.

Output: stretch settings, any test or presentation fix they force, hud_theme.tres, HudRoot, DayCounter, the three font remaps, the GoldLabel outline and test_hud_contract.gd.
</objective>
