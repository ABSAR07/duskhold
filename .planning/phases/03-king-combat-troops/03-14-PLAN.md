## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged as in plans 03-01 to 03-12): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

Gap: G-03-4 (03-UAT.md test 4, severity minor; the same defect as review finding WR-01 in 03-REVIEW.md). After a heal the simulation read 49/50 and the HUD widget 49 / 50 (0.981), but the floating bar over the king still showed 0.960, the last damaged value. Root cause: `presentation/king/king.gd` binds king_damaged, king_downed, king_respawned and dawn_payout but never king_healed, so during a night only a hit refreshes the bar and every regeneration point is missed. `KingWidget` polls `KingState` every frame and is correct. Everything else in test 4 passed (the slam, its cooldown, the regeneration pace).

<objective>
Make the floating bar over the king follow regeneration: listen to king_healed and set the bar to the event's health, so it climbs with the widget and hides again at full health. Pin it with a real-scene test written first, and confirm it in a real window.

Purpose: closes G-03-4 (KING-05 regeneration seen consistently on both health readouts; UI-01). The review ledger row WR-01 is left to the next code-review pass, which re-reads the file.

Output: `_on_king_healed` in `presentation/king/king.gd`, one new test in `tests/e2e/test_king_knockout.gd`, real-window numbers in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
