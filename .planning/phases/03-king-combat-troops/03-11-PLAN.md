## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. Output: the army scenario with fixtures, golden, CLI and CI wiring; bot command support; the army_balanced strategy; unit metrics in the report; the retuned night counts with re-pinned tests; 03-BALANCE-REPORT.md.
</objective>
