## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
The king fights with a weapon. His passive numbers move into a WeaponDef without changing a single digest, the sword gains its slam on a 9 s cooldown, his health regenerates after a second without damage, and the player watches both on a bottom-left widget.

Purpose: KING-04 and KING-05 and their decisions D-17 to D-20 and D-22, with the weapon as data for the meta phases (D-18).

Output: WeaponDef and sword.tres, the KingDef migration, KingState slam/cooldown/regeneration on countdown counters, ActivateAbilityIntent and its reasons, the ability action and controller, SlamVfx, king_ability_used and king_healed, KingWidget with a code-drawn sword icon, and their tests.
</objective>
