## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
The player sends squads anywhere on the visible ground: right-click to move, the attack-move key and a click to fight the way there, P and right-clicks to patrol, Shift to queue, Escape to cancel, with a ping where each order lands and a clear message when a point is not valid. First the owner settles the one real conflict in the locked key list.

Purpose: the rest of UNIT-10 and the D-25 scope amendment (move, attack-move, patrol, Shift queue, mouse ground points), deterministic and replayable (Pattern 6).

Output: the attack-move key decision, move/attack-move/patrol and the queue in SquadSystem, point validation and ground_half_extent, the simulation input-API scan, GroundPick, the mouse point commands, OrderMarkers pings, the new Input Map actions and their tests.
</objective>

2. **Execution in `SquadSystem.step`** (per squad, before unit behaviour): when the head is MOVE or ATTACK_MOVE set the anchor to its point and run it until `SquadOrders.arrived`, then pop it; when the queue empties after a move the resting stance becomes post at that point (D-09: no hold unless ordered). During MOVE units acquire no target (RESEARCH A5). During ATTACK_MOVE and a resting PATROL a unit acquires with `TargetQuery.nearest_enemy(enemies, unit_position, guard_range)` and keeps the target until it dies or is beyond leash_range of the unit's own position (Pattern 3), then rejoins the walk. PATROL's path is `[centroid at the moment the patrol becomes active] + points`, walked waypoint by waypoint in ping-pong order with the anchor set to the current waypoint. `executing_verb` returns attack_move while an attack-move runs, else empty; `resting_stance` returns the stance the squad will rest in once its queue finishes. `orders_of(squad_id)` returns a snapshot (Array of {verb, points}) and `patrol_path_of` a copy; nothing private escapes.
