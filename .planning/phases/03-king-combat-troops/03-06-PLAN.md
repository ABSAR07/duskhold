## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "The king leads an army. The player builds a Barracks and an Archery Range whose squads fight on their own, commands them RTS-style with hotkeys, mouse selection and control groups, and fires the weapon's active ability. All of it is fully playable on keyboard and mouse or on gamepad with a complete in-run HUD."

<objective>
The player commands squads for the first time: click a squad (or its slot), Ctrl-click to add, double-click for every squad of a type, Tab through them or F2 for all, and press F to follow the king, H to hold, R to rally. The selection shows as rings and a highlighted slot; a press with nothing selected says so.

Purpose: UNIT-05, UNIT-06 and the first verbs of UNIT-10 and UNIT-09 on keyboard and mouse (D-09, D-10, D-11, D-14, D-15, D-16, D-25), end to end from the Input Map to the deterministic order state machine.

Output: the order intent and gate, follow/hold/rally in SquadSystem with persistence, squad_ordered, SelectionModel/SelectionMath/SelectionController, SelectionRings, slot selection styling, CommandKeyController, the Toast, the new Input Map actions and their tests, and the ROADMAP overview sync.
</objective>
