## Flagged Assumptions

- The gate pass rule (RESEARCH Pattern 10, D-09): every enemy crosses before T_max = 60 s of simulation (RESEARCH measured 14 to 21 s for 200) and no living enemy, crossed or not, is flagged stuck at the end (so a stall after the gate fails the gate on its own); median and maximum crossing times and the longest stuck streak are reported, not gated.
- The gate scenario uses 300 grunt-sized stress enemies (D-10's stress count, within the shipped per-night cap; the shipped grunt's movement and size, extra health only so 04-09's stress night keeps them on screen) and a stress-only wall of 100000 health per segment, so it measures going around; breaking through is proved by test_wall_behaviour (04-07).
- The harness removes enemies that have crossed and reached the castle (RESEARCH F5's sink) and keeps the king away from the gate; neither touches an enemy that has not crossed.
- Overlay row labels are new and unique ("Walls evaluated", "Fastest breach", "Field version" and so on), because the overlay toggle test flattens every section into one dictionary (02-08 note in STATE.md).
- Numbers in the gate's pass rule, sorted by where they live. Geometry comes from data: the finish line is derived from the stress map's gate_wall run (its line z, 30) and the sink from the map's castle_radius (3.5), so moving the wall or the castle in `tools/stress/stress_map.tres` moves them too and the test cannot drift from the map. The three remaining numbers are pass-rule thresholds of a tools-only, export-excluded harness, not gameplay tunables, so they stay code constants and are exempt from the locked "every tunable number in .tres data" rule: FINISH_MARGIN_M (2.0 m past the line counts as crossed), SINK_MARGIN_M (4.0 m round the castle) and DEFAULT_MAX_SECONDS (T_max, 60 s, RESEARCH Pattern 10, overridable through the bounded --max-seconds flag). The precedent is test_balance_acceptance.gd, which pins the Phase 2 bar (BALANCED_MIN_WINS, SQUAD_VALUE_BAR) in test code. Changing one of them changes what the gate proves, so it is a decision for the owner, not a tuning step.
