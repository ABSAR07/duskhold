## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Nights scale to Thronefall-size battles, with hundreds of units, enemies, and projectiles at 60 fps. Walls and barricades shape enemy paths without gridlock. This lands before any content breadth is added."

<objective>
Teach everyone on the ground to go around walls. Task 1 is the tracer: a nav grid from the wall segments, an integer castle field rebuilt when a segment changes, and enemies that follow it through the gaps (or attack the wall when there is no way round), proved on a fixture with a gap and on the sealed fixture. Task 2 drops targets that stand behind walls, routes squads and the bot king through the gaps on a small goal-field cache, and caps the grid.

Purpose: ENMY-04's "around" half (D-04, D-05); plan 04-07 adds D-07's break-or-detour comparison on top of these fields.

Output: NavTuning and its data, NavGrid, FlowField, GoalFieldCache, the Pathing field calls, the LOS rules in EnemySystem, StructureIndex and TargetQuery, the squad and bot-king routing, the gap fixture and the tests.
</objective>
