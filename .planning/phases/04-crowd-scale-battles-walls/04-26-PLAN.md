<objective>
The second view port of the round (G-04-5; with plans 04-24, 04-25, 04-27 and 04-28 it closes the 04-VERIFICATION.md gap for ROADMAP success criterion 3 by the owner's route of 2026-10-20). EnemyViews is the second-largest view cost (about 1.1 ms of mean frame with bars and puffs): its per-enemy loop moves to C++ over the packed read view that already exists, on the shape plan 04-25 proved. Task 1 is the tracer for this view: a clock seam so the GDScript fill can be pinned deterministically, the pins, a native fill of the bodies and bars, the same pins, a measured gain, then the GDScript loop goes. Task 2 moves the death-puff ring and stops idle batches uploading (review IN-08).

A halt does not stop the round: if a stop rule ends Task 1, EnemyViews keeps its GDScript fill and Task 2 still runs (the puffs and the idle-commit skip do not depend on the enemy fill); if the puff bar fails in Task 2, DeathPuffBatch keeps its GDScript ring while the idle-commit skip and the native entry test still land. Either way the plan ends halted, plan 04-27 still ports the units under its own pins and bar, and plan 04-28 measures whatever shipped and names the halted part and its reason. The same holds when plan 04-25 halted: this plan ports the enemy views on its own pins and bar.

Output: the clock seam, the enemy pins, NativeEnemyViews, NativePuffs, the delegating EnemyViews and DeathPuffBatch, the idle-commit skip, the native entry tests, the bench lines and the screenshot reviews in the SUMMARY.

<exact_port_rules>
Restated in every view-port plan (04-25 to 04-27):
1. Vector2 and Vector3 are godot-cpp types with real_t float; Color components are float; PackedFloat32Array elements are float. Every GDScript float (constants, blend, the flash arithmetic, puff ages, a method result once it leaves the method, PackedFloat64Array elements such as the puff birth times) is double in C++; GDScript int is int64_t.
2. Narrow exactly where GDScript narrows: the lerp weight is cast to real_t; a Vector3 or Color built from doubles narrows each component (the bar height `lift * 2.0 + BAR_CLEARANCE` is computed in double and narrowed once; the flash and the bar ratio are doubles narrowed into the custom Color); a PackedFloat32Array element widens exactly to double. Keep every expression's operation order and grouping.
3. Vector math only through native/src/gd_math.h as its operation table allows (Vector2 lerp is there since 04-15).
4. Visit order is the GDScript's: the read view's ascending ids; the puff ring from its next slot round. A binary search or a merge over ascending ids may replace a dictionary lookup where it gives the same answer.
5. The mirror: write exactly the floats CrowdBatch.add wrote (the three origin floats, the colour when the batch has colours, the custom data; the basis untouched), in the same places; the floats past the count keep what earlier frames left; a native fill starts from the batch's own setup mirror.
6. No FMA and no fast math; only + - * / and sqrt; no time, no randomness, no engine singleton in native/src (the frame milliseconds and the puff clock come in as arguments); test_native_rules_guard enforces it.
7. Pins first, on the GDScript, committed on their own, never edited.
8. One implementation ships: the replaced GDScript per-frame bodies are deleted; no runtime fallback.
9. Goldens: smoke 2599c7c2 (703 ticks), army f7a967ed (1353) and walls bfb05fc7 (1961) against tests/golden, full_idle 4385f569 (6911) twice, the gate digest e448ffdb with GATE_OK 300/300/0. The views only read, so nothing may move; any move stops the plan before the commit.
10. Every native method checks sizes, batch indices and ids that cross from GDScript and answers what the GDScript answered for bad input (nothing drawn, -1, 0, false, the zero vector).
11. GDScript names native classes as types (rule (i) of 04-14-SUMMARY).
12. Constants stay GDScript and are handed over at bind (configure); the one rule copied into C++ is HealthBar3D.should_show (max_hp > 0 and hp > 0 and hp < max_hp), with its source named beside it.
</exact_port_rules>
