## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here, flagged to the orchestrator as in Phases 2 and 3): "Nights scale to Thronefall-size battles, with hundreds of units, enemies, and projectiles at 60 fps. Walls and barricades shape enemy paths without gridlock. This lands before any content breadth is added."

<objective>
Draw every enemy through batched MultiMesh rendering instead of one node per enemy, with the same look, interpolation, hit flash, hurt-only bars and death puffs, and prove with all three replays that presentation never touched the simulation. Task 1 is the tracer: the CrowdBatch helper, its shader and the enemy batches end to end in the real scene with the goldens byte-identical. Task 2 moves the bars and the puffs into batches and checks the night screenshots.

Purpose: the first half of the rendering switch that D-13 orders before any pathing change; RESEARCH F2 measured 866 to 1,144 draw calls for node-per-entity views against 118 to 141 for one batch per archetype (PLAT-01).

Output: CrowdBatch, crowd_instance.gdshader, the rewritten EnemyViews, HealthBarBatch, DeathPuffBatch, their shaders and tests.
</objective>
