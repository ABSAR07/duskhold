## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, no story invented, flagged): "Nights scale to Thronefall-size battles, with hundreds of units, enemies, and projectiles at 60 fps. Walls and barricades shape enemy paths without gridlock. This lands before any content breadth is added."

<objective>
Make a crowd-size night step cheap without changing a single outcome. RESEARCH F1 measured 50 ms per step for 300 marching enemies against a 33 ms step period, almost all of it the every-tick structure scan of target-less enemies; a uniform grid returns the same answers in about 1 ms. Task 1 is the tracer: the structure grid and the cached rescan ticks, proved by the goldens and a bench. Task 2 adds the enemy grid behind TargetQuery, the bucketed push-apart, and the packed read view the enemy batches fill from.

Purpose: PLAT-01's binding constraint is the GDScript step, not rendering (RESEARCH Summary); every later plan's stress numbers depend on this pass, and the existing goldens prove it exact.

Output: StructureIndex, EnemyIndex, CrowdReadView, Separation.displacements_bucketed, the EnemySystem and UnitSystem changes, the EnemyViews fill, the step bench and the equivalence tests.
</objective>
