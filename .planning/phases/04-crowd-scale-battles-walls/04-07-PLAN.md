<objective>

Output: WallDecision, StuckTracker, their NavTuning fields and data, the EnemySystem decision wiring and the tests.

## Flagged Assumptions

- CONTEXT discretion decided: "aggroed to a segment" means an enemy marching on the castle whose straight line is blocked by that segment while it is within wall_notice_range (12 m) of it, or an enemy attacking it; "near" segments are those of the same run within wall_neighbour_span (2) of it.
- D-08's cadence is per segment, staggered as rescans are ((tick + segment) % 15); one pass over the aggroed enemies runs each tick for the due segments (RESEARCH Pattern 9's "one O(n) pass").
- The detour is the castle field's cost to the castle, which already routes through the nearest open gap or hole; enemies with another target are line-of-sight gated (04-06), so the castle is the only goal the comparison needs.
- D-09's N is 3 s and "progress" is a 0.5 m improvement of the castle-field cost; healthy queued enemies can be flagged for a while in a crowd surge (RESEARCH F5: waits up to about 13 s), which is why plan 04-08's gate passes on "everyone crosses before T_max and none is still flagged at the end".
