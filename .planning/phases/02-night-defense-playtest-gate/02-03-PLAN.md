## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Enemies fight back. They pick targets the way D-11 describes (the king can pull them; otherwise they head for the castle), strike the castle and the king, and the king can be knocked out and comes back at the castle after a growing, capped countdown that the player sees (KING-06, D-01 to D-06). Health bars show who is hurt.

Purpose: the night gains real risk to the king's position and the castle; this is the knockout rule set the owner decided at discuss-phase.

Output: CastleState, TargetQuery, D-11 targeting with separation, king health and knockout/respawn, respawn tuning data with its helper, HealthBar3D, the ghost king and the HUD countdown, and their tests.
</objective>
