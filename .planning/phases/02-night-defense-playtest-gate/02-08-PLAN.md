## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Let the player see each night coming (LOOP-02) and keep the day untimed (LOOP-01) now that nights are real; give the night banner its real copy; and extend the debug overlay with live wave state, the king's status and enemy paths (ROADMAP success criterion 3).

Purpose: the telegraph turns the day into a decision about where to build; the overlay lets Claude and the owner read what the simulation is doing during playtests and screenshots.

Output: SpawnTelegraph with its pure placement helper, the night preview line and banner copy, the LOOP-01 regression, NightOverlaySections, EnemyPathGizmo, and their tests.
</objective>
