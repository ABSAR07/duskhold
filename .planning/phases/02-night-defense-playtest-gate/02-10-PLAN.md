## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Build the scripted playthroughs and the balance report that D-18 delegates to Claude, then tune the shipped combat and wave data until the numbers say what D-10 and D-04 ask for: a first run is winnable with good choices, greedy play loses around the middle nights, and the king is sturdy but mortal.

Purpose: the owner plays only one or two runs at the gate, so the gold trade-offs and the difficulty curve must already be measured.

Output: PlaytestStrategies, the defend-nearest-threat king, per-night metrics in ReplayDriver, BalanceReport with its CLI and wrapper, the tuned data, two guard tests, and 02-BALANCE-REPORT.md.
</objective>

report.json shape (the Task 2 acceptance check reads these exact keys): `{"seeds": [...], "runs": [{"strategy", "seed", "outcome", "nights_survived", "gold_earned", "buildings_lost", "king_knockouts", "per_night": [...]}], "summaries": {"<strategy>": {"win_rate", "mean_nights_survived", "min_nights_survived", "max_nights_survived", "median_loss_night", "mean_gold_earned", "mean_buildings_lost", "mean_knockouts"}}}`. `median_loss_night` is the night number on which lost runs ended (0 when the strategy never lost).
