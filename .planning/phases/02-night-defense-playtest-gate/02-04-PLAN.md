## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Buildings take the hits (BLDG-07). Every building gets health from data; enemies attack the nearest thing in their path, including Houses and towers (D-11); a building at zero health collapses into rubble and stays down for the rest of the night (D-12), and the player sees the damage on a hurt-only bar.

Purpose: outer buildings now buffer the castle and losing them costs income, which is the trade-off the loop depends on.

Output: building health in data and BuildingSystem, building targets for enemies, building events, the rubble view with its collapse effect, and their tests.
</objective>
