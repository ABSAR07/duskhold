## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Towers and archers shoot. The tower fires at the nearest enemy in range with a visible projectile (D-13), a ranged enemy type joins the nights from night 4 (D-08), and every attack the simulation makes, including the king's passive strike, is shown on screen (KING-03).

Purpose: the player defends "with the king and basic towers" as the ROADMAP goal says, and the second enemy type makes positioning and tower placement matter.

Output: TowerSystem, ranged enemy attacks and data, the night ramp with ranged groups, SimClock.flight_ticks, ProjectileVfx, richer enemy puppets, and their tests.
</objective>
