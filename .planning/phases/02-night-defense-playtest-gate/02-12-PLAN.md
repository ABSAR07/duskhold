## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

Gap: G-02-1 point 1 (02-UAT.md). Diagnosis: `.planning/debug/playtest-economy-castle-speed-difficulty.md` (gold enters a run only through `starting_gold` and the Houses' dawn income, so a day-1 Tower leaves 0 gold for the rest of the run; a 700-run probe shows +1 gold per dawn turns the tower-first opening from 0 of 10 wins into 10 of 10 while greedy play keeps losing).

<objective>
Pay a base income of gold at every dawn that does not come from a building, so the owner's tower-first opening is no longer an economy dead end. The amount is map data (`MapConfig.base_dawn_income`, script default 0, shipped 1), it is listed in the dawn payout under a reserved castle key so the payout's total still equals its per-spot amounts, and its coin flies to the gold counter from the castle.

Purpose: closes G-02-1 point 1 ("there needs to be some base gold gain at each wave") without giving towers income (D-10).

Output: the base income in MapConfig, RunManager and RunContext with its validate() rules and data value, the castle coin origin in DawnPayoutVfx, and every test that pinned "Houses are the only income" updated on purpose.

Pinned tests this plan updates on purpose (never deleted):
- tests/unit/test_dawn_income.gd: `test_a_dawn_with_no_houses_pays_nothing_and_the_day_still_returns` (line 118) becomes the base-income test, a base-off variant keeps the old guarantee, and the payout dictionaries gain the castle entry (Task 1)
- tests/unit/test_building_damage.gd: `test_a_dawn_after_a_loss_pays_only_the_survivors` gains the castle entry (Task 1)
- tests/integration/test_loop_gold_carryover.gd: the dawn payouts at lines 62, 69 and 79 and the gold-delta test add the base income (Task 2)
- tests/e2e/test_dawn_payout.gd: coin-count expectations include the castle coin (Task 2)
- Left unchanged on purpose: tests/unit/test_prototype_map_data.gd `test_starting_gold_buys_two_houses_or_one_tower_but_not_both` (starting gold stays 4) and `test_tower_has_two_tiers_with_attack_data_and_no_income` (towers still pay nothing, D-10); the file only gains the base-income pin
</objective>
