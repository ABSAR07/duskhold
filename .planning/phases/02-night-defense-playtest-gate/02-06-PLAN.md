## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Close the night into the next day the way the original does: what fell is rebuilt for free (LOOP-04), but a rebuilt building pays nothing that morning and is visibly marked (LOOP-05, D-14). Survivors and the castle are repaired and the king is whole again.

Purpose: "losing a building costs a day of income, not the building itself" is the trade-off that makes defending outer Houses worth it.

Output: rebuild, repair and payout rules in BuildingSystem and RunManager, the buildings_rebuilt event, the rebuilt-model view, the crossed-out coin marker, and their tests.
</objective>
