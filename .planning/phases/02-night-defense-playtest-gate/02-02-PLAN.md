## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
Make the shipped game's nights real. Author the prototype map's three spawn points and eight nights (D-07, D-09, D-10) and widen the ground, and in the same change move every Phase 1 suite that relies on the timed night onto a waveless copy of the map (RESEARCH Pitfall 2), so the suite never goes red. Then harden the data path: MapConfig.validate() learns the night and enemy rules with caps, and contract tests pin the shipped night data.

Purpose: from this plan on, the player who holds start-night on the prototype map meets enemies; a bad wave file cannot hang or flood a night.

Output: authored night data, the waveless helpers and migrated tests, the night validation, test_prototype_nights, test_map_validate_nights, test_night_data_contract.
</objective>
