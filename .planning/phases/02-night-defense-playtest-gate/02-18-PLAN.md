## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

Gap: G-02-14 (02-UAT.md test 14, owner round 2, 2026-10-07): "speed up should be togglable instead of hold to speed up. everything else is pass" (the 12 m/s sprint, braking, the 2x pace, the label and the night-only rule all pass). Diagnosis: `.planning/debug/fast-forward-toggle.md` (the hold is one level read of the action inside `FastForwardController._apply`; a latched switch flipped on a press edge and fed to the unchanged `scale_for` keeps the night-only clamp, the single-writer rule and determinism; probe A showed `Input.is_action_just_pressed` fires once per trigger pull while a per-event check fires on every motion event, and that Godot has no deadzone hysteresis, so a trigger hovering at 0.5 would re-toggle). The same plan closes the fourth review's open WR-01 (leaked controllers in test_fast_forward_rules.gd), which matters more under a toggle: a leaked latched controller would flip on every later test's press.

Orchestrator semantics (owner decision 2026-10-07, recorded in 02-UAT.md): press once on, press again off; the switch resets to off when the night ends (dawn, won, lost) so each night starts at real time; day presses do nothing; same bindings F and left trigger; the label shows while on.

<objective>
Turn the night fast-forward from a hold into a toggle inside FastForwardController only: a latched switch flipped on a press edge while the phase is NIGHT, cleared in the phase_changed that leaves NIGHT, applied through the same `scale_for`; then make one trigger pull count once even when the trigger hovers at its deadzone. Rewrite the fast-forward tests for the toggle, fix WR-01 in the same change, and sweep the hold wording out of the code and test docs.

Purpose: closes G-02-14 with the owner's exact semantics and leaves the HUD, the bindings, the 2x scale, the night-only rule, the single-writer rule and determinism untouched.

Output: the toggle and its unit and e2e tests with WR-01 fixed (Task 1); the trigger re-arm guard, a new input-edge test file and the doc sweep (Task 2).
