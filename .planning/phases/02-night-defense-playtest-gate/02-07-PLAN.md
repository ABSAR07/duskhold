## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim; the ROADMAP line is not in user-story format and no story is invented here — flagged to the orchestrator): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

<objective>
End the run. The castle falling loses the run in that very step (LOOP-06); surviving the eighth night wins it (LOOP-07). A short, frozen collapse beat precedes the Defeat screen (D-17), and the results screen shows the outcome and the run's stats with Play again and Quit (D-15, D-16).

Purpose: the loop now has stakes and an end, which the playtest gate needs.

Output: WON/LOST terminals with the tie rule, RunStats, the run_ended event, outcome-aware replays, the results screen, the castle collapse and loss beat, terminal lighting and input freeze, and their tests.
</objective>
