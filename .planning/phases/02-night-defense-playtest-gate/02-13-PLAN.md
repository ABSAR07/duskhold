## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

Gap: G-02-1 point 3 (02-UAT.md, "the speed up should be at least 1.5x faster too"). The owner answered "both" on 2026-10-06: (a) a faster sprint and (b) a new night fast-forward. Diagnosis: `.planning/debug/playtest-economy-castle-speed-difficulty.md` (no game-speed control exists; map_root.gd:42 passes the raw frame delta to RunContext.advance; the only speed-up is the sprint at 5.0 x 1.6 = 8 m/s).

<objective>
Make the king faster and let the player speed the night up. The sprint goes from 8 m/s to 12 m/s with a stronger acceleration so the king still stops on a plot. A new hold-to-fast-forward input (F or the gamepad's left trigger) runs the game at twice real time during a night, deterministic by construction because it only feeds the fixed-step clock more time per real second; it switches itself off when the night ends, and the HUD shows when it is on.

Purpose: closes G-02-1 point 3 and gives the owner a way through the waiting part of a night.

Output: the sprint data and its updated pins (the source-ratio pin is replaced on purpose), the fast_forward input action, LoopTuning.fast_forward_scale, FastForwardController in the map scene, the HUD indicator, and their tests.

Decision recorded here for the owner (they left it open): fast-forward acts only in NIGHT. By day there is nothing to wait for (no day timer, and riding is what the sprint is for), dawn is two seconds of payout coins, and the loss beat and results screen must run at real time so the 0.6 s grace and the 1.2 s beat mean what they say. A key held across dawn into the next night resumes fast-forward when that night starts (it is a hold, not a toggle). It also works while the king is knocked out, which shortens the wait for his respawn. 02-16 copies this into the owner's packet.

Note: the sprint change moves the defending bot's speed in every balance run (playtest_bot.gd:125 uses the shipped sprint), so the full_idle replay digest changes here; the smoke replay runs on the frozen fixture king and must not move.

Pinned tests this plan updates on purpose (never deleted):
- tests/unit/test_king_movement_config.gd: the source-ratio pin (`SOURCE_SPRINT_MULTIPLIER` 1.6 at line 6, `assert_eq` and `assert_between(1.4, 1.8)` at lines 20-21) is replaced by the owner's 2.4 and the 12 m/s floor; the acceleration test pins 60 (Task 1)
- tests/e2e/test_king_ride.gd: header comment only; its sprint-ratio assertion is data-driven and stays (Task 1)
- tests/unit/test_input_map.gd: GAMEPLAY_ACTIONS and the three binding tables gain fast_forward (Task 2)
- tests/unit/test_loop_tuning_contract.gd: gains the fast-forward data pin (Task 2)
</objective>
