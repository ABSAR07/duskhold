## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (verbatim): "The prototype map plays the full day → night → dawn loop. The player sees each night coming and starts it deliberately, defends with the king and basic towers, rebuilds and collects income at dawn, and wins or loses on a results screen. Seeded nights replay deterministically, and the owner confirms the loop is fun before anything is built on top of it."

Gap: G-02-1 point 2 (02-UAT.md): "castle should also have a simple attack, not too strong though. Maybe takes three shots to kill a grunt and two to kill the ranged units." Diagnosis: `.planning/debug/playtest-economy-castle-speed-difficulty.md` (CastleState is health only and NightSim steps no castle attacker; damage 2 is the only integer giving 3 and 2 shots; skirmishers shoot the castle from 10.5 m; a 1.0 s interval let an empty map survive night 1 in the probe, 1.5 s keeps no_build losing on night 1 in 7 of 10 seeds).

<objective>
Give the castle a simple ranged attack in the deterministic simulation, using the tower pattern: damage 2, reach 11 m, one shot every 1.5 s, arrows at 18 m/s, all map data that defaults to off. Teach the presentation and the balance tooling about the new attacker first (arrows drawn from the keep, castle kills counted), then switch the castle on.

Purpose: closes G-02-1 point 2 and relieves night 3, the one tight night (the probe shows a castle attack with base income turns the owner-like opening into a win while greedy and idle play keep losing).

Output: CastleAttack in NightSim with its data and validate() rules, castle arrows in ProjectileVfx, a kills_castle column in the replay tally and the balance report, and their tests.

Note: depends on 02-12 because both plans edit simulation/defs/map_config.gd, data/maps/prototype_map.tres and tests/unit/test_prototype_map_data.gd. The castle attack changes every shipped-data balance run, so the full_idle replay digest changes here; the smoke replay runs on frozen fixtures with the attack off and must not move.

Pinned tests this plan updates on purpose (never deleted):
- tests/integration/test_balance_report.gd: NIGHT_KEYS gains kills_castle and `test_kills_by_king_and_towers_add_up_to_the_nights_deaths` becomes the king + towers + castle sum (Task 1)
- tests/unit/test_prototype_map_data.gd: gains the shipped castle-attack contract (Task 2)
- Any shipped-copy test whose subject is not the castle and that breaks because the castle now shoots: its deep copy switches the castle attack off, with a comment, and the SUMMARY lists it (Task 2, step 6); design-contract tests on shipped data are never switched off or loosened
</objective>
